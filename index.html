How to play

1. Build homes. People move in when there is housing, food and work.

2. Build farms and shops. Farms feed the shehar, shops earn rupees every day.

3. Own it like a metaverse. Tap any home or shop → Assign owner → pick a resident. They own it: home at night, shop by day, all on the real IST clock. Add residents in code: js/owners.js.

4. Walk the streets. Press Walk. WASD/arrows or the joystick to move, Shift or RUN to jog, drag to look around, 1-4 to emote. Residents stroll with name tags — wave at them.

5. Keep everyone happy. Faith places, parks, schools and hospitals raise Khush-hali. Factories pollute — balance with trees.

6. Watch the meters. Water tanks, substations and food stocks must cover demand.

The sky is real. Day and night follow actual Indian Standard Time — lamps glow when India is dark, festivals shine when India celebrates.

Controls. Map: drag to pan, rotate buttons to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, tap to build or inspect. Walk: drag to look. Search a name (top-left) to find anyone's home. The cow has right of way — always.
