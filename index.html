How to play

1. Build homes. People move in when there is housing, food and work.

2. Build farms and shops. Farms feed the shehar, shops earn rupees every day.

3. Keep everyone happy. Faith places, parks, schools and hospitals raise Khush-hali. Factories raise pollution — balance with trees and parks.

4. Watch the meters. Water tanks, substations and food stocks must cover demand, or people leave.

5. Celebrate. Diwali, Holi, Eid and Republic Day arrive on their own. Tap Celebrate for a boost.

Controls. Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Tap a building card, then tap the map. Tap placed buildings for info. The cow has right of way — always.
