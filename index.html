How to play

A whole city waits. Mahanagar comes pre-built in code — Civil Lines bungalows, a CBD of companies and towers, the Old Mohalla bazaar, a residential colony, factories and farms. Extend it: pick from the dock and tap the map.

Own it like a metaverse. Tap any home or shop → Assign owner → pick one of the four [NPC] residents. Green plates at doors mean ACQUIRED; cream boards mean TO-LET — available for a new owner.

Walk the streets. Press Walk. WASD/arrows or the joystick to move, Shift or RUN to jog, drag to look around, 1-4 to emote. Only four fake humans ([NPC] tags) stroll the city — wave at them; everyone else here is you.

Read the doors. Every deed-able building shows its status at the door while you walk. Tap a door to inspect it, or search a resident's name to visit their place.

Keep everyone happy. Faith places, parks, schools and hospitals raise Khush-hali. Companies bring jobs and cars; factories bring smoke — balance with trees.

Watch the meters. Water tanks, substations and food stocks must cover a growing city.

The sky is real. Day and night follow actual Indian Standard Time — street lamps glow when India is dark, festivals shine when India celebrates.

The toy lens. The map is shot through a Tilt-Shift Miniature lens — a sharp band where you're looking, the world melting into model-blur above and below, saturated paint-box colours, and long-exposure headlight trails at night. Toggle it with the aperture button or T. Come at rush hour (8–11 am, 5–8:30 pm IST) and the toy town packs with traffic — walk mode always runs at real speed.

Controls. Map: drag to pan, rotate buttons to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, tap to build or inspect. Walk: drag to look. The cow has right of way — always.
